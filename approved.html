Supplements

How we screen supplements

Supplements are held to a higher standard than anything else we recommend. These are the four checks every supplement must pass.

  1. 01Clean ingredientsEvery ingredient is named with its dose. No proprietary blends, and no unnecessary fillers, colours or sweeteners.
  2. 02Outside lab testingTesting must be confirmed by someone outside the brand: a third-party certification, an outside approval or a posted lab report. A brand saying “we test” is not enough.
  3. 03Clean safety recordNo recalls, contamination findings or safety warnings that concern us.
  4. 04Good absorptionA form your body can actually take in.
