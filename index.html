Why I built this

I built AÏA because I needed this, and it did not exist.

Somewhere to go when you are taking care of yourself, or trying to work out how. A place you can come exactly as you are.

Because underneath all of it, everyone is looking for the same thing. To feel at home in their own body. To feel like they belong somewhere. Whether you are lost right now or you are not, there is something here for you.

So this is a place to be curious. To read, to try things, to change your mind, to take what you need and leave the rest.

Take your time. Everything here has been vetted to our standard.
